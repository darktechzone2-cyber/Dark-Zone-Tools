Good to know

  • Works best on clear screenshots, scanned documents, and printed text with good contrast.
  • Handwriting, blurry photos, and decorative fonts give weak results — that is a limit of on-device OCR, not a bug.
  • The first run downloads the OCR engine (a few MB). Choosing Urdu also downloads its language model (~1 MB) once; after that, everything is cached and runs offline-capable on your device.
  • Very large images are automatically shrunk to 2000px on the longest side so phones stay fast — small text in huge screenshots may read better if you crop first.
