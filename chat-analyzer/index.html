How to export a chat

  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp.
  2. Tap the chat name, then scroll to Export chat.
  3. Choose Without media and save/share the .txt file.
  4. Upload that file here — or paste its text above.

Works with Android and iPhone exports, 12-hour and 24-hour times, and day-first or month-first dates (auto-detected). System messages (like "Messages are end-to-end encrypted") are counted separately and never assigned to a person.
