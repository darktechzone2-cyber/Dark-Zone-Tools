How the score works

WPM (words per minute) = correct characters divided by 5, divided by the minutes you typed. One standard word counts as 5 characters, so long and short words are scored fairly. Accuracy = correct characters divided by all characters typed. Grades: under 20 WPM Beginner, 20–34 Average, 35–49 Good, 50–64 Fast, 65–79 Very Fast, 80 and above Expert.
