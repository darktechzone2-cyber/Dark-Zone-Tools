How to read the AQI scale

US AQILevelWhat it means
0–50GoodAir is clean. Enjoy outdoor activities as normal.
51–100ModerateAcceptable air. Very sensitive people should watch for symptoms.
101–150Unhealthy for sensitive groupsChildren, elderly and asthma patients should reduce long outdoor exertion.
151–200UnhealthyEveryone may feel effects. Reduce outdoor exertion; sensitive groups should avoid it.
201–300Very UnhealthyHealth alert. Avoid outdoor exertion, keep windows closed, use a mask outdoors.
301+HazardousEmergency conditions. Stay indoors; wear an N95 mask if you must go out.

Data: Open-Meteo Air Quality API (Copernicus CAMS model, CC BY 4.0) and Open-Meteo Weather API. Values are model estimates for your area, updated hourly — nearby streets can differ. This tool is for general guidance, not medical advice. If you have breathing problems, follow your doctor's advice.
