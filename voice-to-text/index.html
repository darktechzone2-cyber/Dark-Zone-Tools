How to use

  1. Choose the language you will speak.
  2. Press Start listening and allow microphone access when the browser asks.
  3. Speak clearly. Grey text is live; finished sentences move into the box.
  4. Say "new line" or "full stop" for punctuation, or edit the text by hand.
  5. Press Stop, then Copy or Download your text file.

Honest limits: voice typing works in Chrome, Edge and Safari — Firefox does not support it. It needs an internet connection because Chrome and Edge recognise speech on their own servers; this page never records or uploads your audio itself, and your text stays in this tab until you copy or download it. Accuracy depends on your microphone, background noise and the language you pick.
