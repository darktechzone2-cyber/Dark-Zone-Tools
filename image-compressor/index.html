DTZ Image Compressor

Shrink your photos for WhatsApp, Telegram and websites. Upload up to 20 images, pick the quality and size, and download real compressed files — everything happens in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

1. Add your images

JPG, PNG or WebP — up to 20 files, 20 MB each. You can also paste an image from your clipboard.

Tap to choose images, or drag them here
JPG / PNG / WebP  •  Max 20 files  •  Max 20 MB each  •  Paste (Ctrl+V) works too

2. Compression settings

Smaller quality = smaller file. Quality 80 is the sweet spot for photos — sharp on a phone screen, much lighter to send.

Auto keeps it smart: photos become JPEG, transparent PNGs become WebP (transparency kept, much smaller), WebP stays WebP. JPEG has no transparency — a transparent PNG converted to JPEG gets a white background.

3. Your compressed images

Tap Download on any image, or grab them all as one ZIP. If a file was already tiny, we keep the original instead of making it bigger.
