How to use

  1. Type or paste your script (up to 1000 characters) and pick the voice language.
  2. Click Generate Voice — each part gets its own audio player.
  3. Press play to listen, or tap Save MP3 on a part to save it, then use the clip in your video editor.

Tip: write short sentences and add full stops where you want a pause — the voice pauses at every full stop. Roman Urdu works best with the Urdu voice; English words inside an Urdu script are read in an Urdu accent, so write names the way they sound. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored anywhere — the text goes straight to Google’s public speech service to make the audio.
