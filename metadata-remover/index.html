Every photo hides data: camera model, the exact date, your GPS location, the app that made it — and AI images even carry their prompt. This tool shows you everything, then removes it. Your pixels are never re-encoded: the image data is copied byte-for-byte, only the metadata blocks are cut out.

You can also paste a copied image (Ctrl+V). JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF · up to 20 files, 25 MB each.

Nothing is uploaded to any server — the files never leave your device. No signup, no key, no account.

What gets removed

  • EXIF — camera make & model, lens, date & time taken, orientation, exposure settings.
  • GPS location — the exact latitude, longitude and altitude where the photo was taken.
  • XMP & IPTC — editing history, keywords, copyright and app tags (Photoshop, Lightroom…).
  • AI & C2PA tags — generator prompts stored in PNG text chunks, and Content Credentials (JUMBF) blocks.
  • Comments & timestamps — JPEG comments, PNG creation time, GIF comments.
Kept on purpose: your image itself, its size and quality, and the colour profile (ICC) so colours stay exactly the same.
Honest limits: HEIC photos (iPhone default) are not supported in the browser yet — on iPhone, share the photo as JPG first, or set Camera > Formats > Most Compatible. Some apps (like WhatsApp) already strip metadata when you send a photo — this tool is for direct sharing, uploads and downloads, where the data survives.
