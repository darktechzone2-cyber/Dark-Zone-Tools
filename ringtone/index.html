    How it works & limits

    • Your song is decoded and cut entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded to any server.
    • MP3 export uses the lamejs encoder (128 kbps by default). If it cannot load, the tool offers a WAV download instead.
    • Some DRM-protected files (e.g. Apple Music downloads) cannot be decoded by the browser and will show an error.
    • On iPhone, use Safari and save the MP3 to Files, then add it as a ringtone via GarageBand or iTunes.
