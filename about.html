About Dark Tech Zone

Dark Tech Zone is a free online tools hub built for the tech community in Pakistan and beyond. Every tool on this site is 100% free — no sign-up, no account, no API key needed. Just open a tool and use it.

What you will find here

  • Downloaders — save public videos and media: Instagram, TikTok, X (Twitter), Facebook, and YouTube thumbnails.
  • AI tools — AI voice generation, AI code assistance, AI caption writing, and AI YouTube title ideas.
  • Everyday utilities — live weather with a 14-day forecast, text-to-speech in many languages, screen recording, a PDF toolkit, a speed test, image-to-text (OCR), and public IP/DNS lookups.
  • Services — free temporary email and free media hosting (Media Cloud).

How the tools work

Most tools run entirely in your browser. Tools that process files (for example the PDF Toolkit and Screen Recorder) process them locally on your device and never upload them to our servers. A few tools use free public third-party services to fetch public data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Who runs this site

Dark Tech Zone is run by Ali and the Dark Tech Zone community. We also run a YouTube channel and Telegram and WhatsApp channels where new tools are announced first.

Contact

Questions, tool requests, or feedback: reach us via our Telegram channel, our WhatsApp channel, or our YouTube channel. You can also use the feedback form on the tools hub.
