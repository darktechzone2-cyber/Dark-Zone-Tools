How it works

Stations come from the free public Radio Browser directory (50,000+ stations worldwide). Press play and the live stream plays right here — nothing is downloaded or recorded. Some stations broadcast on plain HTTP streams, which secure pages cannot play; those are hidden by the “Only stations that play on this page” switch. Turn it off to see them, then use Open Stream to listen in a new tab. Favorites and recently played stations are saved only in your browser.
