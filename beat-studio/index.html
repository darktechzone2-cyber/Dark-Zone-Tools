How To Make Your First Beat

  1. Pick a ready-made style above the drum machine (for example Desi Groove or Hip-Hop), or tap squares to build your own pattern.
  2. Press Play Beat and set the Tempo slider until the groove feels right.
  3. Press Record Melody, play notes on the piano, then press it again to stop. Your melody will loop with the drums.
  4. Choose a length and press Download WAV — your song file is created on your device, ready to share anywhere.
  5. Press Copy Share Link to send your beat to friends. They open the link and hear exactly your pattern and melody.

Honest note: all sounds are synthesized live by your browser (Web Audio). No audio files are downloaded and nothing you play is uploaded anywhere.
