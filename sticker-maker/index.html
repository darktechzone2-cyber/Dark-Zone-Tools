How to use a sticker in WhatsApp

  1. Download your sticker as a .webp file, or download a whole pack as a .zip and unzip it first.
  2. Open any chat in WhatsApp, tap the sticker icon, then tap Create and pick the sticker file. (Availability of “Create” depends on your WhatsApp version.)
  3. Or import the files with a sticker-maker app such as Sticker.ly or Sticker Maker: open the app, create a new pack, add your .webp files, and tap “Add to WhatsApp”.
  4. For a full pack in WhatsApp, a pack needs at least 3 stickers — the ZIP from step 3 gives you all the files ready to import.
